Case study: two niches, familiar problems

Patterns I see over and over in these two industries — and exactly where social media skills apply.

Barbershop

Common social media problems
  • Posting is random — busy weeks mean total silence on the feed
  • Phone photos of finished cuts, no consistent look or template
  • No booking link visible in bio or story highlights
  • Before/after transformations rarely get shown side by side
  • No local hashtags or geo-tags, so nearby customers don't find them

Motorcycle shop

Common social media problems
  • Build process and repair work never gets filmed, only the final result
  • Almost no reels — the format riders actually watch and share
  • Inconsistent branding between posts, story, and shop signage
  • No clear call-to-action for booking service or asking about parts
  • Little engagement with local rider groups and events

Restaurant

Common social media problems
  • Great dishes are being served every day, but the preparation and behind-the-scenes process rarely gets filmed.
  • Almost no Reels - despite food videos being highly visual and easy for customer to discover and share.
  • Inconsistent branding across food photos, stories, Reels, Menus, and promotional posts.
  • No clear call-to-action for reservations, takeout order, inquiries, or location.
  • Little engagement with local customers, food communities events, and nearby businesses.
  • Promotions and seasonal offers aren't turned into cosistent content.
  • Customer reviews and user-generated content aren't being leveraged.
  • Social Media shows the food, but not why customers should choose the restaurant

Solutions & examples

  • Weekly content batchingA single afternoon each week to shoot and schedule everything, so posting never depends on a slow day.
  • Branded Canva template setOne before/after layout, one promo layout, one reel cover — same fonts and colors every time.
  • Link-in-bio + highlight coversBooking link always visible, plus highlights for pricing, hours, and past work.
  • Short-form build videos15–30 second clips of real work in progress, edited from footage shot during the job.
  • Local tagging & community postsGeo-tags, area hashtags, and shout-outs to local riders or regulars.
